# T-M1-10 Kotlin rules engine: schedule evaluation
Milestone: M1 · Depends on: T-M1-09 · FRD: FR-03, FR-05

## Goal
The Kotlin rules engine reads a snapshot and decides whether an app is blocked
by a schedule at a given time.

## In scope / out of scope
In: parse snapshot JSON, evaluate schedules including overnight windows,
days of week, overlapping schedules. Out: daily limits (T-M1-11), website
matching (T-M1-11).

## Files likely touched
android/rules-engine/src/main/kotlin/.../SnapshotParser.kt,
android/rules-engine/src/main/kotlin/.../ScheduleEvaluator.kt (+ tests)

## Acceptance checks
- [ ] Parses the snapshot JSON from test_vectors/
- [ ] 22:00–07:00 schedule: blocked at 23:30, not blocked at 08:00
- [ ] Two overlapping schedules: blocked when either is active
- [ ] Schedule with specific apps: only those apps are blocked
- [ ] Empty schedule list: nothing is blocked
